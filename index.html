  • R&D and engineering delivery

    Thirty years in engineering and technology delivery across R&D, product lifecycle management, global delivery and supply chain. Started in R&D at Robert Bosch, working alongside plant procurement, engineering and quality teams, then engineering and delivery roles at Siemens and Philips.

  • Mahindra Satyam, Geometric & PROLIM Solutions

    Engineering and delivery roles at Mahindra Satyam, then as Client Partner at Geometric carrying P&L ownership for a roughly USD 10M global automotive and engineering portfolio, with a team of about 200. Later led the client relationship and delivery for an enterprise PLM programme at PROLIM Solutions. That was the services side of the table.

  • Building the GCC, Schneider Electric

    As Director of the India GCC, built the case for moving a business line's India work out of a partner-run offshore centre and into a Global Capability Center of Schneider's own — approved by the business line's SVP and CFO and by the India CEO and CFO. The build covered the location and structure decisions, the first SEZ entity in Schneider India, its support functions, the partner agreements and a purpose-built site.

  • Scaling through COVID and beyond

    The centre went live in December 2020, mid-COVID, and grew faster than planned. A Germany site was consolidated into Hyderabad in 2021, and a second centre opened in 2023. Reported to the US business-group VP as the senior link between global headquarters and India, accountable for the centre's operations and governance and for representing it with government agencies, auditors and clients.

  • Extending into global supply delivery

    Alongside the GCC role, took on additional responsibility for Process Automation's supply and delivery worldwide (Mexico factory, customers in every region) as the post-COVID component shortage pushed lead times up, becoming a full-time focus with a move to Boston in 2024. Aligned R&D, procurement, manufacturing, quality and sales around one view of what was critical, as the single point of contact for delivery escalations across more than 20 suppliers and 20 customers.
